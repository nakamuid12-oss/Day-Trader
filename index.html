Historical setups

The first candle to close beyond the opening range sets the entry reference. The stop is the opposite side of the range and the target is the reward : risk multiple. Quantity is illustrative: it ignores costs, slippage, liquidity, circuit limits, lot sizes and capital availability. The rule has not been validated for profitability. Verify every price with a broker or exchange-approved source, and use only data you are licensed to use.
